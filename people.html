Who Kalyan Setu Is For

Different lives, same need — good food.

We are building for those who keep life moving. Across India, millions of people spend their days away from home — in colleges, on construction sites, behind steering wheels, in factories. They deserve nourishing, affordable meals just as much as anyone else.

01

People Facing Food Insecurity

People who may struggle to afford even basic nourishing meals.

Food security is not a given for many individuals. Kalyan Setu begins with them.

02

Students

Affordable food for students managing limited budgets and life away from home.

Hostel food is often inadequate. Outside food is expensive. Students deserve better.

03

Workers

Accessible nourishment for people working long hours and managing everyday expenses.

A worker who skips a meal to save money loses energy and dignity both. We intend to change that.

04

Drivers & Daily Workers

People who spend most of their day working outside and often depend on affordable food options.

Those who keep cities moving often can't afford to stop and eat a proper meal.

05

People Living Away From Home

Affordable and dependable food for people who cannot easily access home-cooked meals.

Distance from home should not mean distance from a wholesome meal.

06

Low-Income Families

Food solutions designed with affordability and everyday dignity in mind.

Dignity at the table is not a luxury. Every family deserves it.

If you belong here — or know someone who does — Kalyan Setu is being built for you.
